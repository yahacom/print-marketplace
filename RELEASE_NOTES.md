# Release 0.2.0

You can now go from an uploaded 3D model to a confirmed order, start to finish.

**Get an instant price.** Upload an STL file and the marketplace slices it for real and gives you a price based on print time and material — not a rough guess. You'll also see the model's size and a 3D preview right on the quote screen, so you can check it's the right file before you commit.

**Confirm or decline with your contact details.** Once you have a quote, tell us your name, phone, email and how many you'd like, and confirm the order — or decline it if it's not what you need. Each quote can only be decided once, so there's no risk of double orders.

**More reliable behind the scenes.** Upload, quoting and ordering are all now rate-limited and monitored, so the service stays responsive even under heavy use.

---

Known limitations in this release: there's no account system yet, so a quote can't be reopened after you reload the page — keep the tab open until you've confirmed or declined. Order confirmation hasn't yet completed its security review.
